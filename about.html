About Give and Reward More

Who we are, why we exist, and how we approach our work with children in Uganda.

Who we are

Who We Are

Give and Reward More is an organization based in Uganda, working to support approximately 35 children with the essential needs that give them a fair chance at a healthy, stable childhood: shelter, food, clothing, medical care, education, and school supplies.

Further detail on our history and founding will be added here once confirmed by the organization.

Mission & vision

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To help meet the essential needs of children in our care — shelter, food, clothing, healthcare, and education — and to connect them with a community of supporters who share that commitment.

Final mission statement wording to be confirmed by Give and Reward More.

Our Vision

A future where every child we support has the safety, health, and education needed to grow into their full potential.

Final vision statement wording to be confirmed by Give and Reward More.

What guides us

Our Values

Dignity

Every child is treated with respect, and their privacy is protected in everything we publish and share.

Transparency

We aim to be clear and honest about our work, our needs, and how support is used.

Community

We believe lasting support comes from genuine partnerships with families, supporters, and local organizations.

How we work

Our Approach

We work directly with the children in our care to understand their needs, and we coordinate support across categories such as shelter, nutrition, healthcare, and education. We prioritize working with verified information and avoid overstating our impact.
