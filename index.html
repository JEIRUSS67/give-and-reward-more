Our purpose

Every Child Deserves a Chance to Thrive

Give and Reward More exists to help children in Uganda access the things every child needs to grow up safe, healthy, and educated: a place to sleep, regular meals, clothing, medical care, and the chance to go to school. We work closely with the children in our care and the wider community to understand what is needed most, and to connect that need with people willing to help.

Our approach is built on transparency and respect for every child's dignity. We do not treat the children we support as subjects of a story — they are the reason this organization exists.
