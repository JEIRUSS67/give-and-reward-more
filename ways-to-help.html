Ways to Help

There are several ways individuals, organizations, and partners may be able to support Give and Reward More. These are presented as proposed options; not all may currently be accepted, pending confirmation by the organization.

Financial Support

Monetary contributions to support ongoing needs across shelter, food, healthcare, and education.

Food Donations

Contributions of food or nutrition support, subject to the organization's current logistics.

Clothing & Supplies

Clothing, hygiene items, and other everyday material donations.

Education Support

Support for school fees, learning materials, or educational programs.

Medical Support

Support toward medical treatment, supplies, or professional healthcare services.

Volunteering

Contributing time and skills directly to support the children and the organization's work.

Professional Services

Legal, medical, educational, or other professional expertise offered on a voluntary basis.

Corporate Partnerships

Companies and organizations partnering with Give and Reward More on an ongoing basis.
